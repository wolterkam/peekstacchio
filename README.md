# Peekstacchio

---

KWin script that won't let your open windows disappear behind other apps.

Keep track of what's open on your desktop without relying on a taskbar (or your memory).

## What does it do?

When activating a window fully obscures another window, the covered window moves smoothly to peek out from behind the active window. It's still there, just one click away.

## Limitations

Windows can't peek out from behind a maximized or fullscreen window.
